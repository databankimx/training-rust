project to build in VS Code, starting from `cargo new`. Once you've tried an exercise, reveal its example solution in the runner, and compare.

1. **Rejecting a post.** Add a `reject` transition to the blog from section 18.3, with tests.
2. **Shapes as trait objects.** Hold different shapes in one collection, and total their areas.
