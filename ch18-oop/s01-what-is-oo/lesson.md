## 18.1 Characteristics of Object-Oriented Languages

Object-oriented programming has no single agreed definition. This sub-section looks at three common features, objects, encapsulation, and inheritance, and shows what Rust does with each.

* [Objects and encapsulation](#goto/01-encapsulation): structs with methods, and private fields that keep an average up to date.
* [Reusing behavior without inheritance](#goto/02-reuse-without-inheritance): default trait methods, overriding them, and the book's quiz.
