## Chapter 14: More About Cargo and Crates.io

So far we've used the basic features of Cargo: building, running, and testing. Cargo can do more, and this chapter covers the advanced features that help a project
grow and share its code.

The chapter is split into sub-sections:

* [14.1 Customizing builds with release profiles](#goto/s01-release-profiles): the `dev` and `release` profiles, and overriding a setting in `Cargo.toml`.

Later sections will cover publishing libraries to crates.io, organizing projects with workspaces, installing binaries, and extending Cargo with custom commands.
