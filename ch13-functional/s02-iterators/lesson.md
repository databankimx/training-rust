## 13.2 Iterators

Iterators produce a sequence of items on request. This sub-section covers how to create and step through one, how consuming methods use an iterator up, and how
adapters build new iterators from old ones, with closures.

* [Creating and using iterators](#goto/01-creating-and-using): `iter`, `next`, and `for` loops.
* [Consuming and adapting](#goto/02-consuming-and-adapting): `sum`, `map`, and `collect`.
* [Closures that capture their environment](#goto/03-filtering-with-closures): `filter`, and a closure that uses a value from its surroundings.
* [Summary](#goto/04-summary): the main ideas of this sub-section.
