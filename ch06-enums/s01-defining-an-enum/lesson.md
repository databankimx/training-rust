## 6.1 Defining an Enum

An enum says a value is one of a set of possibilities. This sub-chapter shows how to define an enum, how its variants can hold
data, and how `Option<T>` uses the same idea to replace `null`.

* [Enum variants](#goto/01-enum-variants): defining an enum, and using its variants as values.
* [Variants with data](#goto/02-variants-with-data): variants that hold their own values, of different types.
* [The Option enum](#goto/03-option): representing a value that may be absent, and why it's safer than `null`.
