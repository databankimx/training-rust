## 19.3 Pattern Syntax

Patterns can match literals, ranges, and alternatives, bind names with `@`, and destructure structs and other values. A match guard adds a condition to an arm.

* [Literals, ranges, and bindings](#goto/01-literals-and-ranges): literals, `|`, `..=`, `_`, and `@`.
* [Destructuring structs and match guards](#goto/02-destructuring-structs): struct patterns, `..`, and `if` guards.
* [Summary](#goto/03-summary): the main ideas, and the book's quizzes.
