## 8.4 Ownership Inventory #2

The second Ownership Inventory uses two programs that change a vector while working with it. The quizzes check how well you can tell which
borrows are still in use, and which fixes keep the function's intent.

* [Example: Removing zeros from a vector](#goto/01-removing-zeros): a loop that removes elements while iterating over them.
* [Example: Reversing a vector in place](#goto/02-reversing): two mutable borrows of one vector, and the standard library's answer to them.
* [Lessons from the inventory](#goto/03-summary): the common thread between the two programs.
