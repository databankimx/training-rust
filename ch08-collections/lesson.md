## Chapter 8: Common Collections

Collections store many values in one place. Unlike arrays and tuples, their size can change while the program runs, and the values they hold live on
the heap. This chapter covers the three collections that appear most often in Rust code: vectors, strings, and hash maps.

The chapter is split into sub-chapters:

* [8.1 Vectors](#goto/s01-vectors): creating and changing a list of values, reading and iterating over it, and storing mixed types with an enum.
* [8.2 Strings](#goto/s02-strings): creating and appending, concatenating, why strings can't be indexed by number, and iterating over text.
* [8.3 Hash maps](#goto/s03-hash-maps): keys with associated values, ownership, updating with `entry`, and grouping.
* [8.4 Ownership inventory #2](#goto/s04-inventory): two programs that change a vector while working with it, and how to fix them.
