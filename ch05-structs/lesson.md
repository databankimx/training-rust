## Chapter 5: Structs

A struct groups related values under one name, so each piece of data has a clear meaning. Structs are the first way
you'll build your own types in Rust, and they are where ownership, borrowing, and methods start to work together.

The chapter is split into sub-chapters:

* [5.1 Defining structs](#goto/s01-defining-structs): defining, creating, and changing struct instances, and borrowing their fields.
* [5.2 An example program using structs](#goto/s02-example-program): refactoring a rectangle-area program from separate numbers to a struct.
* [5.3 Method syntax](#goto/s03-method-syntax): defining methods on structs, and how they borrow or take ownership of the instance.
