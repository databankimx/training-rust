## 5.1 Defining Structs

This sub-chapter introduces structs: how to define one, create an instance, and read or change its fields. It also covers
shorter ways to write instances, tuple and unit structs, and how borrowing works on individual fields.

* [Defining and instantiating](#goto/01-defining-and-instantiating): the `User` struct, dot notation, and `mut`.
* [Shorthand and update syntax](#goto/02-shorthand-and-update-syntax): shorter ways to build instances.
* [Tuple and unit structs](#goto/03-tuple-and-unit-structs): structs without named fields, and structs without any fields.
* [Borrowing fields](#goto/04-borrowing-fields): how the borrow checker tracks each field separately.
